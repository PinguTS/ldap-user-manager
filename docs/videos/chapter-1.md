# Chapter 1 — Your new account: setting your first password

**Audience:** Anyone who received a new-account invite email (regular org user or new org admin).  
**Length:** about 1–2 minutes.  
**Stop after:** “Go to login” — do not log in as a regular user.

**Prep:** Have a fresh invite email open (create the user with “Email a link so the user sets their own password” checked). Use a demo mailbox, not a real person’s inbox on camera.

---

## Action script

| Cue | Do this |
|---|---|
| 1 | Show the invite email (subject/body mentioning set password / activate account). |
| 2 | Click the password-set link in the email. Browser opens `/password/set/?token=…`. |
| 3 | Point at the card title **Set your password** and the account label. |
| 4 | Enter a new password in **New Password**; re-type in **Confirm**. Pause so the strength meter reacts. |
| 5 | Click **Set password**. |
| 6 | Show the success message; click **Go to login**. |
| 7 | Stop on the login page (optional brief pause). Do **not** sign in. |

---

## Narration

**Cue 1.** When your organization creates an account for you, you receive an email with a link to set your password.

**Cue 2.** Open that email and click the link. It takes you straight to the password setup page. You do not need to log in first.

**Cue 3.** Check that the account shown on the page is yours.

**Cue 4.** Choose a new password, then confirm it. Watch the strength indicator — pick something strong enough for the site’s rules. Avoid single or double quotes in the password.

**Cue 5.** Click Set password.

**Cue 6.** When you see the success message, click Go to login.

**Cue 7.** Your password is ready. Day to day you use this account with your organization’s other services. You only come back here if you need to set or reset a password from an email link.
