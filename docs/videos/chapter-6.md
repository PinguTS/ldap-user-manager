# Chapter 6 — Org admin: user status and the Manager role

**Audience:** Organization administrators.  
**Length:** about 1–2 minutes.  
**Start:** Logged in on `/manage/organizations/{uuid}/users/`.  
**Prep:** A non-manager user you can deactivate; another user you can toggle Manager on (not the only remaining manager).

---

## Action script

| Cue | Do this |
|---|---|
| 1 | On the user list, point at the **Status** column (switch) for a normal user. |
| 2 | Turn the Status switch **off** (deactivate). Show the success banner / inactive state. |
| 3 | Turn the Status switch **on** again (activate). |
| 4 | Point at the **Manager** column. Toggle Manager **on** for a suitable user. Show success / row highlight. |
| 5 | Toggle Manager **off** again (unless you want them to stay a manager). |
| 6 | Hover or point at a manager row: note Delete is disabled for managers, and you cannot remove the last manager. |

---

## Narration

**Cue 1.** Status controls whether someone can sign in. The switch in the Status column activates or deactivates that person.

**Cue 2.** Turn the switch off to deactivate. They stay in the list but cannot log in until you turn it back on.

**Cue 3.** Turn the switch on to activate them again.

**Cue 4.** The Manager column marks organization managers — people who can administer this organization. Flip the switch on to assign the role.

**Cue 5.** Flip it off to remove the manager role when you no longer need them as an admin.

**Cue 6.** You cannot delete a manager from this list, and you should keep at least one manager. The interface blocks removing the last manager so your organization is never left without an admin.
