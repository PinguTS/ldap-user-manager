# Chapter 4 — Org admin: organization at a glance & editing company details

**Audience:** Organization administrators.  
**Length:** about 1–2 minutes.  
**Start:** Logged in as org admin on the organization page.

---

## Action script

| Cue | Do this |
|---|---|
| 1 | On `/manage/organizations/{uuid}/`, focus the **Organization Information** table (name, description, address, phone, fax, website, email). |
| 2 | Point at **Statistics** (total users, user limit, roles). |
| 3 | Point at the Actions row: **View users**, **Add user**, **Edit organization**. Do not click Deactivate (out of scope). |
| 4 | Click **Edit organization**. The edit form scrolls into view. |
| 5 | Change one or two safe fields (e.g. phone and website). Leave organization name alone (org admins cannot rename). |
| 6 | Click **Update Organization**. |
| 7 | Show the success banner and the updated Information table. |

---

## Narration

**Cue 1.** Your organization page shows the company profile — name, address, phone, website, and email.

**Cue 2.** Statistics summarize how many users you have and whether a user limit applies.

**Cue 3.** From Actions you can open the full user list, add a user, or edit the organization. Leave status actions like Deactivate to platform staff.

**Cue 4.** Click Edit organization to open the form.

**Cue 5.** Update the details your members should see. Keep the organization name as it is — renaming is not available to organization admins.

**Cue 6.** Click Update Organization to save.

**Cue 7.** Confirm the success message and that the information table shows your changes.
