If you forgot your password, start at the login page.

Click Forgot your password.

Enter the email address for your account, then click Send reset link.

For privacy, the site always shows the same message — if that address is in the directory, a reset email is on the way.

Open the email and click the link. You land on Reset your password.

Choose a new password, confirm it, and submit.

Click Go to login when you are done. Your organization administrator can also send you a reset link from the user list, using New password — that is covered in the org admin videos.
