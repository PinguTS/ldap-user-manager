Status controls whether someone can sign in. The switch in the Status column activates or deactivates that person.

Turn the switch off to deactivate. They stay in the list but cannot log in until you turn it back on.

Turn the switch on to activate them again.

The Manager column marks organization managers — people who can administer this organization. Flip the switch on to assign the role.

Flip it off to remove the manager role when you no longer need them as an admin.

You cannot delete a manager from this list, and you should keep at least one manager. The interface blocks removing the last manager so your organization is never left without an admin.
