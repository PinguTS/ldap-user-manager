When your organization creates an account for you, you receive an email with a link to set your password.

Open that email and click the link. It takes you straight to the password setup page. You do not need to log in first.

Check that the account shown on the page is yours.

Choose a new password, then confirm it. Watch the strength indicator — pick something strong enough for the site’s rules. Avoid single or double quotes in the password.

Click Set password.

When you see the success message, click Go to login.

Your password is ready. Day to day you use this account with your organization’s other services. You only come back here if you need to set or reset a password from an email link.
