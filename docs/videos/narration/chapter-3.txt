As an organization administrator, sign in with your email and password on the login page.

After you log in, you go straight to your organization’s page. That is your home screen for this role.

Here you can see company details, user counts, shortcuts to manage people, and a short list of recent users. We will cover those next. For now, note that this page is where you start after every login.

To update your own password while signed in, open Change password in the menu.

The page explains the password rules. The easiest path is Generate Password — it creates a strong password and fills both fields for you. You can also type your own.

Click Change password. When you see Success, your new password is saved and your session stays valid.

You are ready to manage your organization. Log out when you are finished, or continue with the next chapter from your org page.
