# Chapter 2 — Forgot or reset your password

**Audience:** Anyone who needs a new password without being logged in.  
**Length:** about 1–2 minutes.  
**Note:** An org admin can also start a reset with **New password** on the user list (Chapter 7).

**Prep:** Know a demo user’s email that exists in the directory. Have the reset email ready to open after you submit the form.

---

## Action script

| Cue | Do this |
|---|---|
| 1 | Open `/login/`. Leave language on English. |
| 2 | Click **Forgot your password?**. |
| 3 | On **Forgot your password?**, enter the demo email in **Email**. Click **Send reset link**. |
| 4 | Show the generic confirmation (“If the address exists, a password reset link has been sent.”). Optionally click **Back to login**. |
| 5 | Open the reset email; click the reset link → `/password/set/?token=…` with card **Reset your password**. |
| 6 | Enter **New Password** and **Confirm**; click **Set password**. |
| 7 | Click **Go to login**. Stop — do not sign in as a regular user. |

---

## Narration

**Cue 1.** If you forgot your password, start at the login page.

**Cue 2.** Click Forgot your password.

**Cue 3.** Enter the email address for your account, then click Send reset link.

**Cue 4.** For privacy, the site always shows the same message — if that address is in the directory, a reset email is on the way.

**Cue 5.** Open the email and click the link. You land on Reset your password.

**Cue 6.** Choose a new password, confirm it, and submit.

**Cue 7.** Click Go to login when you are done. Your organization administrator can also send you a reset link from the user list, using New password — that is covered in the org admin videos.
