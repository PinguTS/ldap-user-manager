# Chapter 3 — Org admin: first login & change your own password

**Audience:** Organization administrators.  
**Length:** about 1–2 minutes.  
**Accounts:** Org admin only (no system users).

---

## Action script

| Cue | Do this |
|---|---|
| 1 | Open `/login/`. Enter the org admin username/email and password. Click **Log in**. |
| 2 | After login you land on `/manage/organizations/{uuid}/` — your organization page. Pause on the org name and breadcrumbs. |
| 3 | Briefly scroll the page: Organization Information, Statistics, Actions, Recent Users (do not click Delete on Recent Users). |
| 4 | In the top navigation, click **Change password**. |
| 5 | On **Change your password**, mention the rule bullets. Click **Generate Password** (fills both fields). |
| 6 | Click **Change password**. Show the **Success** card. |
| 7 | Optional: click **Log out**, or end on Success. |

---

## Narration

**Cue 1.** As an organization administrator, sign in with your email and password on the login page.

**Cue 2.** After you log in, you go straight to your organization’s page. That is your home screen for this role.

**Cue 3.** Here you can see company details, user counts, shortcuts to manage people, and a short list of recent users. We will cover those next. For now, note that this page is where you start after every login.

**Cue 4.** To update your own password while signed in, open Change password in the menu.

**Cue 5.** The page explains the password rules. The easiest path is Generate Password — it creates a strong password and fills both fields for you. You can also type your own.

**Cue 6.** Click Change password. When you see Success, your new password is saved and your session stays valid.

**Cue 7.** You are ready to manage your organization. Log out when you are finished, or continue with the next chapter from your org page.
